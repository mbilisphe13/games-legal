Blockwave (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a block puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

The short version

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. There is no account and no sign-up. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The app does show advertising, and our advertising partner collects some data to do that.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device’s own storage:

  • Your high score, games played and total lines cleared
  • Your daily challenge streak and results
  • Your current game, so you can close the app and resume
  • Your sound on/off preference

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, it is not sent to any server, and it is not backed up to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

Blockwave is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising ID
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with the ads shown to you

We never receive this information ourselves. It is collected by Google under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Your choices about ads

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. You can also turn off ad personalisation there.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads, and you can change your choice later.

Children

Blockwave is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will act on it.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is used only to load advertising. It does not request your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or precise location.
